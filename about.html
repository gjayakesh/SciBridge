About SciBridge

Not a course. A launch pad.

SciBridge exists because the hardest part of a great space project was never the idea — it was finding the right two or three people, and someone who had done it before, willing to help.

Real Projects

Every project on SciBridge is built to be shown to a university, a lab, or a space organization — not graded and forgotten.

Real Mentors

Engineers, scientists and researchers guide projects after admin approval. They advise — they never take ownership.

Real Teams

Students, engineers and researchers from anywhere in the world, working in one shared workspace.
